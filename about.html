About YMCA Sierra Leone

Discover the organisation, values and hospitality that are part of YMCA Sierra Leone.

Who we are

Young people, working together

YMCA Sierra Leone is an ecumenical Christian organisation enhancing the capacity of young people in Sierra Leone through collaborative approaches for self-reliance and holistic development. The YMCA movement is a global confederation of 130 national movements, founded in England in 1844, serving over 40 million young people. YMCA Sierra Leone was established in 1912 to provide programmes that address the body, mind and spiritual upliftment of young people.

Our vision

“Empowering Young People for the African Renaissance.”

Hospitality at the YMCA

The YMCA Hostel is in the heart of Freetown, about a 10-minute walk from the market and main shopping centres, on the second and third floors of the administrative building. It welcomes all nationalities and communities: men, women, families, students, trainees and tourists.

Facilities

  • WiFi in all rooms
  • Cafeteria with veg and non-veg meals, take-away and special menus on request
  • TV lounge on the first floor
  • Indoor recreation facilities within the complex
  • Laundry arranged through reception; electric iron available at reception
  • Prepaid phone cards sold at reception and pay-and-use email
  • Limited car parking
  • Major shops and supermarkets within walking distance
